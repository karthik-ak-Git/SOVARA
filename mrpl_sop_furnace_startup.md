# MRPL CCR Furnace F-101 Pre-Startup Safety Checklist

## 1. General Requirements

This procedure outlines the mandatory safety checks required prior to initiating startup sequence for Continuous Catalytic Reforming (CCR) Furnace F-101 following a turnaround.

## 2. Nitrogen Purging and Gas Testing

- Nitrogen Purge Verification: Verify that all furnace sections are purged with nitrogen to ensure oxygen levels are below the permissible limit.
- LEL Gas Testing: Conduct continuous LEL gas testing throughout the furnace system. The permissible LEL criteria must be strictly maintained below 0.5%.

## 3. Fuel Gas System Integrity Checks

- Fuel Gas Valve Train Leak Check: Perform a double-block-and-bleed leak check on the entire fuel gas valve train to ensure zero leaks prior to introducing fuel.
- Line Integrity: Verify all associated piping and valves are free from mechanical damage or leaks.

## 4. Pilot Burner Ignition Sequence

- Pilot Preparation: Ensure pilot system is fully prepared, pressurized, and verified for stable operation.
- Ignition Sequence: Follow the established, documented sequence for pilot burner ignition, monitoring all pressure and flow parameters continuously.

## 5. Burner Flame Scanner Permissives

- Flame Scan Verification: Before introducing main fuel flow, verify that the burner flame scanner indicates a stable, proper flame pattern consistent with the pre-startup specifications.
- Permissive Check: Ensure all required safety interlocks and permits for initiating the main fuel flow are in place and confirmed by authorized personnel.

## 6. Final Pre-Startup Review

- All checks listed above must be documented and signed off by the designated shift supervisor before startup is permitted.
