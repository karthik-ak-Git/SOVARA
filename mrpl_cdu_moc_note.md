Date of Submission: [Insert Date]

Description of Change: Replacement of Crude Distillation Unit overhead condenser tube bundle from Carbon Steel to Titanium Grade 2.
Justification: Mitigation of wet H2S sour water corrosion.

## Process Hazard Analysis (PHA) Summary
Summary of PHA review indicates that the proposed change addresses a significant corrosion risk associated with the existing Carbon Steel material exposed to wet H2S sour water. The replacement material is selected based on superior resistance properties, ensuring process safety and asset integrity are maintained or improved.
Risk Assessment: Low residual risk post-implementation due to material upgrade.

## Metallurgy Comparison
| Property | Carbon Steel (Existing) | Titanium Grade 2 (Proposed) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Primary Application | General service, moderate temperature |
| H2S/Sour Water Resistance | Poor, susceptible to sulfide stress cracking |
| Corrosion Rate | High risk of wet H2S corrosion |
| Operating Temperature Limit | Moderate |
| Suitability for Service | Unsuitable for sour wet service |

## Environmental Compliance
The change aligns with MRPL's environmental standards by reducing the potential for material failure and subsequent release of contaminants. All new materials must comply with relevant local and national environmental regulations concerning material handling and disposal.
Compliance Status: Compliant pending final regulatory review.
